    All data comes from your own server (stream/), which subscribes to Solana program logs and decodes every swap on Pump.fun (including graduated tokens on the Pump AMM), Raydium LaunchLab (Stonk.fun, LetsBonk and other platforms) and Meteora DBC (Bags and others). Each ripple is one real swap, sized by its USD value. Top volume ranks by 24h volume, Trending by recent momentum, and Both shows the two lists side by side. "Genuine only" hides pools with red flags: liquidity pulled, wash or self-trading, active mint or freeze authority, a pool holding almost all supply, an implausible market cap, or dust liquidity. Liquidity is the USD value of the quote-side reserve. Volumes and price changes count from when the server started (up to 24h).
