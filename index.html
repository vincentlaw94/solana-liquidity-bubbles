    All data comes from your own server (stream/), which subscribes to Solana program logs and decodes every swap on Pump.fun (including graduated tokens on the Pump AMM), Raydium LaunchLab (Stonk.fun, LetsBonk and other platforms) and Meteora DBC (Bags and others). Each ripple is one real swap, sized by its USD value. Top volume ranks by 24h volume, Trending by recent momentum, and Both shows Trending, Top volume and Breakout side by side (drag the lines between them to resize, click a list's title to collapse or show it). Drag a column's right edge to resize it. Breakout ranks coins that pass safety checks (genuine trading, under 20% bundled, no bundler deployer, $3k+ liquidity, $5k–$2M market cap, not dumping, not up over 5× this hour) by room to grow, buy pressure, volume pace, traders, pool depth and curve progress. It is a screen, not a prediction; its record in the side panel shows how flagged coins actually did. "Genuine only" hides pools with red flags: liquidity pulled, wash or self-trading, active mint or freeze authority, a pool holding almost all supply, an implausible market cap, dust liquidity, or a launch 75% or more bundled. Liquidity is the USD value of the quote-side reserve. Volumes and price changes count from when the server started (up to 24h).
